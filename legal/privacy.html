Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Infinity ("we", "us", "our") builds a social platform for developers. This policy explains what information we collect when you use Infinity, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account info — username, email address, password (hashed), and optional profile details (bio, avatar, links).
  • Content you create — posts, comments, votes, saved bookmarks, and code snippets.
  • Uploaded media — images attached to posts or your profile.
  • Usage data — device type, browser, IP address, pages viewed, and general interaction logs.

2. How We Use It

  • To create and secure your account, and authenticate you when you log in.
  • To operate core features — feeds, voting, bookmarks, snippets, jobs listings.
  • To personalize what you see and recommend content or developers to follow.
  • To detect abuse, spam, and violations of our Terms of Service.
  • To communicate with you about updates, security notices, or support requests.

3. Code & Snippets

Snippets you post are treated the same as any other content you share publicly on Infinity. Please don't paste API keys, credentials, tokens, or other secrets into a snippet — once shared, that content is visible to other users and may be cached, indexed, or copied outside our control. We are not responsible for exposure of secrets included in content you choose to publish.

4. Sharing With Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited data with service providers who help us run Infinity — for example, hosting, analytics, or error-tracking tools — under agreements that limit how they can use it. We may also disclose information if required by law.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you logged in, remember your preferences, and understand how Infinity is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though some features may not work correctly if you disable them.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time from Settings, or by contacting us directly. Some information may be retained where required for legal or security purposes.

7. Children's Privacy

Infinity is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can remove it.

8. Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll notify you through the app or by email before the changes take effect. Continued use of Infinity after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach out through our Contact page.
