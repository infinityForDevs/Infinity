Infinity started because every place developers already hang out makes them choose one mode at a time — Reddit for talk, GitHub for code, Facebook too broad. We wanted a feed that doesn't force the split.

A snippet, a hot take about a framework, and a debug journey can sit in the same scroll, because that's genuinely how a working day feels. No pivot to "professional mode," no separate app for "just code."
